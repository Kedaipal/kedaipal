Set up HitPay for your Kedaipal store

Give buyers a one-tap Pay now button — DuitNow QR, Touch 'n Go, FPX and cards — with payments confirmed automatically.

🧰 What you need

  • Your SSM registration (business registration number)
  • The IC of the owner/director (for verification)
  • Your business bank account (where payouts land)
  • About 20 minutes — plus 1–3 working days for HitPay to approve your account

🎁 What you get

  • One-tap Pay now on every order page — buyers pay in their own banking or eWallet app
  • Automatic confirmation — no more "I've paid" screenshots, no more checking your bank app
  • Money lands in your own bank account — Kedaipal never touches it
The plan, in one line: Create your HitPay account (A) → get verified (B) → switch on your payment methods (C) → copy your API Key & Salt (D) → paste them into Kedaipal (E) → do one RM1 test (F). Done.

💡 Important to know first

The HitPay account is yours, not Kedaipal's. Buyers pay you; HitPay takes a small fee per transaction (around 1.2%–2%) and pays the rest into your bank account. Kedaipal takes nothing from your sales.

A
Step A · about 5 minutes

Create your HitPay account

HitPay is a payments company used by thousands of Southeast Asian businesses. A free account gives you the DuitNow QR / TnG / FPX / card rails and the API keys Kedaipal uses to create your checkout pages.

1

Go to hitpayapp.com and sign up

On your phone or laptop, open hitpayapp.com and tap Sign up. Choose Malaysia as your country — this decides which payment methods you can offer.

2

Confirm your email

HitPay emails you a confirmation link. Tap it, set your password, and you're into the dashboard.

⚠️ Pick Malaysia, not Singapore

HitPay started in Singapore, and the sign-up sometimes defaults there. A Singapore account can't offer DuitNow QR / TnG / FPX to Malaysian buyers — make sure the country says Malaysia before you continue.

B
Step B · 10 minutes + 1–3 working days for approval

Get verified (KYC)

Before HitPay lets real money flow, they must verify your business — this is a Bank Negara requirement, the same check every payment company does. In the dashboard, look for the verification / activate your account banner and submit:

1

Your business details

  • SSM registration number (Enterprise or Sdn Bhd both work)
  • Business name exactly as registered
  • What you sell (e.g. "food & beverages — cakes and pastries")
2

The owner's identity

A photo of the owner/director's IC (front and back) and sometimes a selfie. This is standard — it proves the account belongs to a real person.

3

Your payout bank account

The bank account where HitPay deposits your money. Use your business account if you have one — the account name should match your SSM registration.

💡 While you wait for approval

Approval usually takes 1–3 working days. You can already do Steps C and D in the meantime — just hold the RM1 test (Step F) until the account is approved.

C
Step C · about 3 minutes

Switch on your payment methods

In the HitPay dashboard, open Settings → Payment Methods and turn on the ways you want buyers to pay. Kedaipal automatically detects what you've enabled and shows exactly those to your buyers — nothing more, nothing less.

1

Recommended for Malaysian buyers

  • DuitNow QR — works with every MY banking app · fee ≈ 1.2%
  • Touch 'n Go eWallet — fee ≈ 1.9%
  • FPX — online banking transfer · fee ≈ 1.8% + RM0.40
  • Cards — Visa / Mastercard · fee ≈ 1.2% + RM1 (local)

Fees are charged by HitPay per transaction and can change — the dashboard shows the current rate next to each method. There's no monthly fee.

2

Add your logo and brand colour

Open Settings → Checkout Customisation and upload your store logo + pick your colour. Buyers see your brand on the payment page — not Kedaipal's, not plain HitPay.

⚠️ Some methods need extra approval

FPX and cards may ask for extra documents or a short review before they switch on. DuitNow QR and TnG usually work first. Start with what's available — you can add the rest later, and Kedaipal picks up changes automatically.

D
Step D · about 1 minute

Copy your API Key & Salt

These two codes are how Kedaipal creates checkout pages on your behalf. In the HitPay dashboard, open Settings → API Keys — the API key and the salt sit side by side on that page.

You are copying exactly two things

API Key a long code — this identifies your account
Salt a second long code — this proves messages really come from HitPay

🔒 Treat these like your bank password

Anyone with these codes can create payment pages in your name. Only ever paste them into Kedaipal → Settings → Payments. Never send them by WhatsApp or email — not even to Kedaipal support (we never ask for them).

E
Step E · about 2 minutes

Paste the keys into Kedaipal

1

Open Kedaipal → Settings → Payments

Scroll to the Online payments card (the one with the HitPay logo).

2

Paste both codes and tap Connect

Kedaipal checks the key with HitPay on the spot. When it works, the card shows Connected plus the payment methods you enabled in Step C. If it says the key was rejected, re-copy both codes — a missing character is the usual culprit.

3

That's the setup done

From now on, every unpaid order page shows a Pay now button. When a buyer pays, the order flips to Payment received by itself and the buyer gets the confirmation on WhatsApp — you don't touch anything.

F
Step F · about 3 minutes · after HitPay approves your account

Do one RM1 test

1

Place a small test order in your own store

Open your storefront link, order the cheapest item (or create a temporary RM1 product), and open the order page.

2

Pay it for real

Tap Pay now, pay with DuitNow QR or TnG, and watch the order flip to Payment received on its own — usually within seconds.

3

Refund it from the HitPay dashboard

In HitPay, find the payment and refund it. (Refunds always happen in the HitPay dashboard — there's no refund button in Kedaipal.)

⏱️ Payouts are not instant — this surprises everyone once

The buyer's app says "paid" instantly, but HitPay transfers the money to your bank in 2–3 working days (T+2/T+3). That's normal for every payment gateway. Don't panic when the RM1 isn't in your bank the same evening.

Quick rules

✅ Do

  • Keep your bank/QR details in Kedaipal too — buyers can still pay by manual transfer, and it's your backup
  • Check the HitPay app/dashboard for your payout schedule
  • Add new payment methods any time — Kedaipal detects them automatically
  • Use Pause in Kedaipal if you ever want to switch Pay now off temporarily — your keys are kept

❌ Don't

  • Don't share your API key or salt with anyone
  • Don't mark gateway payments received by hand — they confirm themselves
  • Don't use a Singapore HitPay account for a Malaysian store
  • Don't expect the payout the same day — T+2/T+3 working days is normal

Common questions

How much does this cost?

HitPay has no monthly fee — only a small per-transaction fee (about 1.2% for DuitNow QR, 1.9% for TnG, 1.8% + RM0.40 for FPX). Kedaipal charges nothing on top. On a RM50 order paid by DuitNow QR, you receive about RM49.40.

Who holds my money?

HitPay collects from the buyer and pays your bank account directly in 2–3 working days. The money never passes through Kedaipal.

Can buyers still pay by manual bank transfer?

Yes. The order page keeps a "Paid by bank transfer instead?" option, and your saved bank/QR details still work exactly as before. Pay now is an extra door, not a replacement.

A buyer wants a refund — where do I do it?

In the HitPay dashboard (find the payment → refund). Kedaipal shows the payment reference on both your order page and the buyer's — quote it to find the payment fast.

I don't have SSM registration — can I still use this?

Not yet — HitPay's verification requires a registered business (a Bank Negara rule, same for every gateway). Your manual bank transfer + QR flow keeps working as always, and you can connect later once registered.

What happens if I change my HitPay password?

Nothing — the API key and salt are separate from your login. Only if you regenerate the keys in HitPay do you need to paste the new pair into Kedaipal (use Replace keys on the card).

Final checklist

Tick these off and you're live:

  • HitPay Malaysia account created and email confirmed
  • Verification submitted (SSM + IC + bank account) and approved
  • Payment methods switched on (DuitNow QR at minimum)
  • Logo + brand colour set in Checkout Customisation
  • API key + salt pasted into Kedaipal → Settings → Payments, card shows Connected
  • RM1 test order paid and auto-confirmed, then refunded

Stuck on HitPay's side?

Account approval, payout, refunds, fees → HitPay support (chat bubble in their dashboard, or support@hit-pay.com).

Stuck on Kedaipal's side?

Keys rejected, Pay now not showing, order not confirming → message us on WhatsApp — we'll check it with you.
